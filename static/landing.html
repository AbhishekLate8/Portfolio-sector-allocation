What it does

Three views into the same portfolio.

Track holdings

Pull in your positions and keep a running record of what you hold, at what weight, updated as you trade.

Visualize by sector

See exposure rolled up by GICS sector instead of by ticker — the view that actually explains your risk.

Spot drift early

Get flagged when a sector creeps past your target weight, before it quietly becomes half the portfolio.

How it works

From raw holdings to a clear picture.

Add your positions

Enter holdings manually or import them — ticker, quantity, and cost basis is all it needs.

Sectors get mapped

Each ticker is matched to its sector automatically, and weights are computed against your total portfolio value.

Review the breakdown

Check the allocation chart, compare it to a target mix, and rebalance with actual numbers instead of a hunch.
